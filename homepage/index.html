About Me

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate in the College of Information Sciences and Technology at Penn State University, advised by Prof. Jinghui Chen. I received my M.S. in Computer Science from the Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences at New York University in 2023, and my B.Eng. in Software Engineering from Nankai University in 2020, where I worked with Prof. Shenglin Zhang and Prof. Yongqian Sun.

Research Interests: My research focuses on language models, particularly post-training for reasoning and agentic capabilities, as well as model steering and reliability through internal representations.

I am currently open to Research Scientist opportunities in industry, including internships and full-time roles. Please feel free to reach out!
